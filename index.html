EMP Gaming (YouTube @empgaminges, TikTok @empgamingtv) tells the stories of video games as series. QUAD is the desktop tool our creators use to publish the videos they make — long videos and shorts — to their own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook from one screen.

How it works

  • The creator connects each account with the platform's official login.
  • QUAD prepares the video with its title, description, hashtags and cover.
  • Before posting to TikTok, QUAD shows the connected account and lets the creator choose who can see the video, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure, with TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and a preview.
  • Nothing is published until the creator presses Publish.
