This policy explains what data QUAD, the publishing tool of EMP Gaming, handles and how.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok: with the creator's permission (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): the account's open ID, display name, username and avatar, and the posting settings returned by the Content Posting API (available privacy levels and interaction settings). QUAD uses them only to show who is posting and to offer the right options.
  • Videos and texts the creator chooses to publish.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform after the creator logs in.

2. How we use it

Only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts when the creator presses Publish, and to show the result. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

QUAD runs on EMP Gaming's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally, encrypted with the operating system's data protection (Windows DPAPI), and are never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them. Videos stay on that computer and are sent only to the platform where they are being published.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the creator disconnects QUAD or revokes access from the platform, and are then deleted. Anyone can request deletion of stored data at empgamingcontact@gmail.com; it is deleted within 30 days.

6. Children

QUAD is not intended for users under 18.
