QUADTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

QUAD ("the App") is the publishing tool of EMP Gaming. It lets EMP Gaming's creators upload the videos they produce to their own social media accounts — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook — from a single desktop interface.

1. Who can use QUAD

QUAD is offered to creators working with EMP Gaming. To use it, a creator connects their own accounts through each platform's official login (for TikTok, Login Kit). QUAD only acts on the accounts the creator has authorised and only when the creator presses Publish.

2. What QUAD does

  • Prepares each video with its title, description, hashtags and cover.
  • Shows, before posting to TikTok, the connected account, the privacy options the creator can choose, the interaction settings, the commercial content disclosure and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
  • Uploads the video through each platform's official API (TikTok Content Posting API).

3. Creator responsibilities

Creators are responsible for the content they publish and must comply with the terms and community guidelines of each platform, including TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Music Usage Confirmation and Branded Content Policy, and must disclose commercial content when applicable.

4. Third-party platforms

QUAD is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook. Use of those platforms is governed by their own terms.

5. Revoking access

Creators can disconnect QUAD at any time from QUAD or from their platform settings (in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). After disconnection, QUAD stops acting on the account.

6. Disclaimer

QUAD is provided "as is". EMP Gaming is not liable for interruptions of third-party services.
